Straight answers

Questions, sorted

Is it actually free?
Yes. We build your website and host it for nothing. There's no setup fee and no monthly charge for the site. You only pay if you choose to add optional extras like your own domain or automatic SMS — and those are billed by the week.
What's the catch?
No catch. We make our money when you choose to add paid extras down the track — and you only do that if the site's already earning you work. If you just want the free site, that's completely fine.
I'm not techy at all. Is it hard?
There's nothing for you to do. You don't build anything, log into anything or learn any software. You tell us your trade, we build it, and we run it for you. If you can send a text, you can do this.
Do I own my website?
It's yours. Your business name, your photos, your content. If you ever want to leave, there's no lock-in holding you back.
How long does it take?
Usually about two days from the time you tell us your trade. We text you a link, you have a look, and we tweak anything you want changed.
Can I cancel?
Any time, with a single text. The extras are billed weekly and there's no contract — so you're never stuck paying for something you don't want.
