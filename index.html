Visible to AI.

AI assistants are becoming the new Google for recommendations. Gemini, ChatGPT and Perplexity cite businesses they can verify — structured data, authoritative mentions, consistent profiles. TACTIC 03 / 06

Keywords that convert.

Not just high volume — searches that end in bookings.

Volume is vanity. "Electrical wiring" gets searched often, but "emergency electrician Penrith tonight" gets booked. We find the high-intent phrases — job type, urgency, location, cost — and build pages that rank for exactly those terms, so your traffic is made of people ready to hire.

TACTIC 04 / 06

The map pack.

Top 3 local results. Right above everything else.

When someone searches for a tradie near them, the first thing they see is a map with three businesses — not paid ads, not organic links, the map pack. Getting in that box comes down to how well your Google Business Profile is optimised, your review velocity and citation consistency. We handle all of it.

TACTIC 05 / 06

Suburb pages.

Rank in every area you service, not just where you're based.

Google ranks pages, not businesses. If you service ten suburbs but only have one homepage, you rank in one suburb. We build a dedicated page for each area — with the job types, local context and search terms specific to that location. Each page is a new door for customers to walk through.

TACTIC 06 / 06

Schema — Google's language.

Speak to Google directly, not through guesswork.

Schema markup is structured data embedded in your page — it tells Google and AI systems exactly what your business is, where it operates, what it charges, what reviews it has and how to contact you. It unlocks rich results, AI citations and map pack authority. This very site uses it.
