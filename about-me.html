About me
D

Hey, I'm Dan.

I'm 17, I live in Melbourne's bayside, and — unfortunately — I have to pay rent. So this isn't a hobby or a school project. Complete Digital is how I make my living, which means your website actually working is the whole job. If it doesn't bring you customers, I don't eat. Simple as that.

I'm a hard-working, community kind of guy. I grew up around tradies and small business owners, and I've watched too many good ones get burnt by agencies — paying thousands for a slow, outdated site, then waiting weeks for an email back. When you work with me, there's no account manager and no hand-offs. The person who built your demo is the person who answers the phone. That's me.

Most trade websites out there are honestly bad. Slow to load, painful on a phone, built years ago and abandoned. I started Complete Digital to break that pattern — build it properly, keep it fast, keep improving it, and charge fairly while I'm at it. That's why I build your site before I ever ask you for a cent: you should see exactly what you're getting first.

Being young cuts both ways, I know. But it means I've grown up on the internet — I know what makes a site feel sharp in 2026, I move fast, and I've got everything to prove. Give me a go, and I'll work harder on your website than anyone else would.
