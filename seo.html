01

Research

We find the keywords your customers actually type — not the industry jargon you'd use, but "emergency electrician near me" and "switchboard upgrade cost." We map the searches with real intent and real volume in your suburbs, size up who's already ranking, and pick the fights worth picking. Everything downstream is built on this list.

02

On-page

The unglamorous foundations that Google reads first: page titles and meta descriptions written to earn the click, clean heading structure, descriptive alt text on every image, and structured data so your reviews, prices and service areas can show up right in the results. Done properly, it's the difference between page one and page nowhere.

03

Local

Trades win or lose in the local map pack. We get your Google Business Profile fully optimised, keep your name, address and phone consistent across directories (citations), and build dedicated suburb pages so you show up for "electrician [your town]" across every area you actually service — not just the one you're based in.

04

Content

Google can't rank a service it can't see. So each thing you do gets its own page — proper service pages that answer real questions, plus project case studies with before-and-afters that prove you've done the work. It earns rankings and reassures the customer in the same move, which is exactly what good content should do.

05

Authority

Google trusts sites that other trusted sites point to. We earn links from trade directories, suppliers, local partners and the associations you're already part of — the real, relevant kind, never spammy link farms. It's slower than the shortcuts, but it's the part that makes rankings stick instead of evaporating after the next update.

06

Monitor

SEO is never "done." We track rankings, traffic and — the only number that pays the bills — conversions. When a page slips or a competitor moves, we see it and course-correct. You get plain-English reports on what's working and what's next, not a dashboard full of vanity metrics. Steady, measured, accountable.
