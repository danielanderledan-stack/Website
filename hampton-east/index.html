Hampton East — a suburb that's coming into its own

Hampton East sits 14 kilometres south-east of Melbourne's CBD, within the City of Bayside local government area. It's bounded by Hampton to the west, Brighton East to the north, and Highett and Moorabbin to the east. The suburb developed primarily in the 1950s — the post-war blonde brick houses that line its streets have a quiet, old-world charm, and the suburb retains a genuinely family feel that the surrounding, more expensive suburbs have gradually traded away for density and development.

The suburb gained national exposure in 2023 when The Block filmed its series there, bringing renovated homes and camera crews to Hampton East's residential streets. Beyond the TV exposure, the series underscored something locals already knew: Hampton East has good bones. Period character homes, wide streets, family-oriented community infrastructure, and easy access to Hampton's beach and Hampton Street village make it a legitimate Bayside address rather than a consolation prize for those who couldn't afford the suburbs to its west and north.

Hampton East doesn't have its own major shopping strip, but it doesn't need one. Hampton Street is within easy walking or cycling distance. Westfield Southland in Cheltenham handles major retail needs. The suburb borrows infrastructure from its neighbours rather than duplicating it, which keeps it functional and liveable without requiring the commercial density that erodes residential character. For businesses operating out of Hampton East, that means the customer catchment extends naturally into the surrounding suburbs — Hampton, Brighton East, Highett and Cheltenham — without needing to cross a significant geographic or demographic boundary.

The Hampton East customer and what they're searching for

Hampton East residents are predominantly families in established homes. They use tradies regularly — maintenance, renovation, landscaping and property services are constant needs in a suburb of mostly owner-occupied houses. They use local professional services — GP, dentist, physio, childcare, tutoring. They book local fitness, recreation and hospitality. And they find almost all of these things through Google, either directly or by checking a referred business online before they call.

That search behaviour is what local SEO is built for.
