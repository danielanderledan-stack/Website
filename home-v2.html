02 — Our rules

Three rules. No fine print.

These aren't marketing lines — they're how the business is structured. Break any of them and you have every right to walk, which is exactly the point.

01

Rule oneNo upcharges. Ever.

The website itself is free to build and free to keep. If you add extras, they're flat weekly prices listed publicly on our pricing page — the number you see is the number you pay.

No setup fees, no "hosting renewals", no surprise invoices in March. If it's not on the pricing page, it doesn't cost anything.

02

Rule twoUnlimited changes.

New phone number, new photos, a page for a new service, a price update before the weekend — send a text, it gets done. Usually same-day, always free.

Your business changes constantly. A website that costs money to update is a website that goes stale. Ours don't.

03

Rule threeYou own everything.

Your domain is registered in your name. Your content is yours. Billing is weekly with no contract, so "cancel anytime" means this Friday, not in 24 months.

If you ever leave, you take the lot with you — domain, files, photos, the works. No exit fees, no hostage negotiations.
