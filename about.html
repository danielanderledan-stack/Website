About

About Complete Digital.

Complete Digital is a web design agency in Bayside Melbourne. We build fast, conversion-focused websites for tradies and local businesses — and then we keep them working. Founder-led, based in Black Rock, working with clients across Melbourne and Australia.

01

Who we are

Complete Digital started in 2025, founded by Daniel Anderle out of Black Rock in Melbourne's bayside. It's a founder-led studio — no account managers, no hand-offs to a junior you never meet. The person who plans your site is the person who builds it and the person who answers the phone. Small on purpose, so the work stays sharp and the client list stays personal.

02

What we believe

A website should earn its keep. For a tradie or a local business that means one thing: more of the right people calling. So every site Complete Digital builds is measured against that, not against how many awards the homepage could win. Fast to load, easy on a phone, honest about what you do, and built to turn a visitor into a booking.

03

Why Bayside

Being local is part of the offer. Complete Digital is based in Black Rock and works across Brighton, Sandringham, Hampton, Beaumaris and the wider bayside — close enough to sit down with you in person rather than only ever trading emails. Knowing the area and the customers in it shapes a better site than a template ever could.

04

How we work

Listen first, build mobile-first, ship something fast, then keep improving it. Complete Digital handles the design, the build, the search visibility and the upkeep as one job, so nothing falls through the gap between a designer, a developer and an SEO. You can see the full method on our design philosophy and SEO approach pages.
