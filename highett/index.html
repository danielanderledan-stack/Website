Highett — a suburb remaking itself

Highett is 16 kilometres south-east of Melbourne's CBD, bounded by Hampton East and Cheltenham to the west and south, Moorabbin to the east, and Sandringham to the north-west. Its eastern boundary follows the line between the City of Bayside and the City of Kingston, meaning the suburb genuinely straddles two local government areas — a fact that matters for how local businesses should structure their SEO.

For most of the twentieth century, Highett was defined by its industrial character and a prominent landmark: four large gasometers near the intersection of Nepean Highway and Bay Road. The gasometers are gone now — replaced by apartments and the mixed-use development that has gradually transformed Highett Road from a basic shopping strip into Highett Village, a precinct of cafes, boutique retailers, restaurants and contemporary apartment buildings. The transformation accelerated substantially after 2011, when the former CSIRO research facility on Highett Road was redeveloped, adding density and commercial activity to the suburb's eastern spine.

The Waves Leisure Centre on Turner Road — an Olympic-length pool built in 1964 and extensively refurbished in the 1990s — remains one of Highett's most used community facilities, drawing residents from across the southern suburbs. It's a reference point for anyone who grew up on the bayside fringe and a reminder that Highett has community infrastructure that older and wealthier neighbours don't always match. Westfield Southland in adjacent Cheltenham provides the major retail anchor for the area, drawing shoppers and foot traffic from across the southern suburbs.

The Highett business opportunity online

Highett is one of the more affordable bayside suburbs, which creates a specific opportunity for the businesses operating here. The customers are a mix of long-term residents in established houses and newer residents drawn by the Highett Road redevelopment and relatively accessible property prices. That demographic mix means a broader range of potential customers than the more homogenous, high-income suburbs to the west — and a local search market that is less saturated than Brighton or Hampton.

For a tradie or service business in Highett, the online competitive environment is genuinely less crowded.
